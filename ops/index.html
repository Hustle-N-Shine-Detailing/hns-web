Rules that protect the account

Keep these consistent.
  • One-vehicle trial before committing to recurring volume.
  • Partner supplies keys, vehicle location, stock/RO number and approved service level.
  • Condition photos at intake when damage or heavy soil is present.
  • Redo requests must be reported within 24 hours of delivery.
  • No work beyond the approved scope without written/text approval.
  • Partner controls their retail price and customer communication.
  • Never promise same-day volume until capacity is confirmed.

Fast outreach scripts

Ask for the used-car/recon manager, service manager, body-shop manager, or owner.

Dealer / Used Lot

Hi, I’m Joshua with Hustle & Shine Detailing. I handle mobile recon overflow for local vehicle businesses. I’m not trying to replace your detail department — I’m looking to be the call you make when inventory stacks up or you need a vehicle frontline-ready fast. I’ll do one vehicle at wholesale as a trial. Who handles recon or used-car operations?
