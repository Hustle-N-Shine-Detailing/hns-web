Privacy & website analytics

We use the information you voluntarily provide in a quote or booking request (such as name, phone, email, vehicle, and service details) to respond, schedule your service, and provide customer support.

Website visit counts

Our website uses a first-party, privacy-conscious visit counter that records events such as pages viewed and booking-button clicks, without storing a visitor's raw IP address in our events table. These counts are separate from Google Analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, this site loads Google Analytics 4 to understand which public pages and referral sources help customers find us. Google Analytics may use cookies and process usage data, such as browser, pages visited, and approximate location, according to Google's privacy terms. We do not send names, phone numbers, email addresses, or quote details as Analytics events. We do not load the Google Analytics tag until you choose to allow it.

You can decline optional Analytics and still request quotes, call, text, or schedule service. You can change your decision at any time using the button below or the analytics privacy settings link in the footer. Declining stops future Google Analytics measurement from this website and attempts to clear this site's Google Analytics cookies.
